10×10 Pop Up Canopy Tent — FAQs

More buyer topics: FAQ hub · MOQ · Shipping

Is 10×10 the same as 3×3 m?

They are commonly paired equivalents in folding tent lines, but exact outside dimensions depend on the frame series—request the spec sheet for the SKU you intend to stock.

Can I reorder sidewalls without frames?

Yes, when the frame family stays the same. Share prior order references so panel hooks and zippers stay compatible.

Do you support sea and air export?

Yes—factory packing lists include weight and cube for freight forwarders; share incoterms and destination preferences in your RFQ.

How is this different from the aluminum folding tent page?

This page focuses on the 10×10 pop up canopy tent size intent and kit composition. Material deep dives live on aluminum folding tent, while umbrella OEM positioning sits on custom canopy tent manufacturer.

How do 30/40/50 series frames relate to a 10×10 footprint?

The footprint (e.g., 3×3 m / 10×10 ft) describes coverage; 30/40/50 square-tube families describe connector strength and tubing grade. Quote both so we match stock or custom programs correctly.

What is the fastest way to get pricing and production timing?

Send quantities, destination, accessory list and artwork stage—baseline replies typically land within ~24 hours when specs are complete. Review MOQ and lead time, then request a quote.
