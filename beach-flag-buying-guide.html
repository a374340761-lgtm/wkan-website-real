Beach Flag Buying Guide

Compare beach flag shapes, pole systems, bases, printing options and quotation information for outdoor advertising projects.

How to use this guide

This guide helps buyers compare product options, printing requirements, packing choices and quotation factors before requesting a factory quotation.

Which shape should buyers choose?

Feather flags provide a tall curved profile with strong visibility. Teardrop flags hold a compact tensioned shape that performs well for concise branding.

What materials are used for beach flags?

Beach flags commonly use printable polyester fabrics matched with fiberglass or aluminum-fiberglass poles. The exact fabric and pole set should be confirmed by size and outdoor use.

Can beach flags be printed double-sided?

Double-sided printing can be requested when brand visibility is needed from both directions. It affects fabric construction, weight and quotation.

How should bases be selected?

Cross bases, water bags, ground stakes and plate bases suit different surfaces. Tell WaiKwan whether the flag will be used on grass, pavement, indoor floors or temporary event sites.

What affects quotation price?

Price is affected by quantity, size, material choice, print coverage, accessories, packaging, sample requirements and shipping method. A complete inquiry helps WaiKwan recommend a configuration before quoting.
