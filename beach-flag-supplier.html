Buyer FAQ

What materials are used for beach flags?

Beach flags commonly use printable polyester fabrics with fiberglass or aluminum-fiberglass pole systems. The exact material should match flag size and outdoor use.

What is the difference between feather flags and teardrop flags?

Feather flags have a taller curved profile with more vertical branding space. Teardrop flags keep a compact tensioned shape for concise messages.

Can beach flags be printed double-sided?

Yes. Double-sided printing can be requested when visibility from both directions is important, and it affects material construction and quotation.
