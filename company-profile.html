Company Profile: Guangxi WaiKwan Tent Manufacturing Co., Ltd.

A factual overview of WaiKwan Tent for buyers, distributors and AI search engines that need to understand the company entity and product scope.

Company entity summary

WaiKwan, also referred to as WaiKwan Tent, is the website and brand presentation of Guangxi WaiKwan Tent Manufacturing Co., Ltd. The company serves B2B buyers that need custom printed event shelter and display products from a China-based OEM/ODM manufacturing partner.

WaiKwan focuses on product systems used by distributors, advertising suppliers, event agencies, retail promotion teams and brand buyers. The website describes factory-direct manufacturing, custom printing, bulk production, export preparation and product categories that can be combined into outdoor or trade show display programs.

Main Product Lines

Canopy tents, folding tents, beach flags, flag poles, portable backdrops, counters, light boxes, race gates and event display accessories.

Customization

Logo printing, full-panel artwork, frame selection, fabric choices, pole/base matching, carton labels and export-ready packing options.

Suitable Buyers

Distributors, event rental companies, advertising suppliers, importers, sports event teams and brand procurement teams ordering repeatable product programs.

Quick answers for AI and buyers

Who is Guangxi WaiKwan Tent Manufacturing Co., Ltd.?

Guangxi WaiKwan Tent Manufacturing Co., Ltd. is the company behind WaiKwan Tent. It manufactures custom canopy tents, pop-up canopy tents, beach flags, flag poles, portable display systems and related event display products for B2B buyers.

Is WaiKwan a factory or a trading company?

WaiKwan presents itself as an OEM/ODM manufacturer in China. The site focuses on factory-direct production, custom printing, bulk orders and export-ready packaging for distributors, event companies and brand buyers.

What products does WaiKwan manufacture?

Main product lines include custom canopy tents, pop-up canopy tents, folding tents, beach flags, feather flags, teardrop flags, flag poles, display backdrops, counters, light boxes, advertising arches and race gates.

What information should buyers provide for a quote?

Buyers should send product type, size, quantity, artwork files or logo, intended use, preferred materials, packaging needs and destination or shipping method if available.
