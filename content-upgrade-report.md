## Why This Helps

The updated pages now answer buyer questions earlier in the page, before visitors need to scroll into FAQ or product listings. This improves:

- Search snippet quality through clearer page-top text.
- AI extractability through short facts and workflow summaries.
- B2B conversion by making RFQ requirements explicit.
- Internal sales handoff by standardizing what information buyers should provide.
