How to order a custom canopy tent with sidewalls and printing

  1. Confirm the footprint and units. Send the width and depth in metres or feet, plus the venue's height limit. Common catalog footprints include 3 × 3 m, 3 × 4.5 m and 3 × 6 m. Ask us to confirm the exact dimensions of the selected frame.
  2. Choose a frame for the intended use. Compare aluminum folding tent frames with the available tent series, and describe how often the tent will be transported and set up.
  3. Specify each printed surface. List roof panels, valances, full sidewalls and half walls. For each wall, state whether graphics are needed on one side or both, and whether doors or windows are required.
  4. Prepare the artwork and approval requirements. Send your logo and layout, then request the template for the selected model before finalizing artwork. Review our artwork file guide and color matching guidance.
  5. Request the complete delivered-order specification. Include quantity, bags, anchoring accessories, replacement graphics, destination and event date. Confirm MOQ, sample or proof approval, production lead time and shipping separately in the quotation.

Check size units before comparing quotes: 16 × 16 inches is 40.64 × 40.64 cm; 16 × 16 feet is about 4.88 × 4.88 m. These are different requirements. For a footprint outside the listed series, ask us to confirm availability rather than assuming a standard model.

Aluminum profiles reduce carry weight and resist corrosion for repeat tours; steel tubes can lower unit cost for seasonal or rental-heavy fleets. If you need heavy duty pop up tent performance, share expected wind conditions, anchoring method, and duty cycle so we can recommend connector grades and ballast plans.

Custom Canopy Tents — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for buyers; full detail in our FAQ library.

What sizes are available for custom canopy tents?

We supply common commercial footprints including 3×3 m, 3×4.5 m, and 3×6 m styles among others. Exact availability depends on frame series—use tent category listings or ask for a matrix during quoting.

Can you print logos on all canopy panels?

Roof and valance branding is standard for many custom printed canopy tents; sidewall printing may vary by fabric and seam layout. Share your layout early—we outline bleed and safe zones in our artwork FAQ.

Do you offer aluminum and steel frames?

Yes. Aluminum suits frequent transport; steel can reduce unit cost for budget-led programs. Specify intended use when contacting us so we recommend the right pop up canopy tents frame.

What is the MOQ for custom canopy tents?

MOQ depends on print scope, fabric, and accessories. See MOQ guidance and include quantity targets in your inquiry for an accurate line item.

Do you support OEM orders for distributors?

We operate as an OEM canopy tent manufacturer for B2B partners—private-label packaging and repeat-order consistency can be discussed with our team via contact.

Can sidewalls and accessories be included?

Yes—sidewalls, weights, and related hardware can be quoted with your frame. Mention venue requirements (e.g., fire retardancy) when requesting printed event tents packages.

What is a pop up canopy tent?

A collapsible frame system with a tensioned fabric roof (and optional walls) that deploys quickly for outdoor branding. We manufacture OEM/ODM frames with custom graphics for promotions, trade events, and sports programs.

Can you produce a custom tent with logo and full-color artwork?

Yes—roof, valance, and sidewall panels can carry custom printed canopy tent graphics or a focused custom tent with logo layout. Submit vector files and color references; review color matching and artwork files for production-ready output.

Do you offer 10×10 custom canopy tents for export?

Yes—10×10 (commonly paired with 3×3 m) is a high-volume format for channel partners. MOQ and lead time depend on printing and accessories; see the dedicated 10×10 pop up canopy tent page and MOQ FAQ.

Can sidewalls be customized separately from the canopy?

Yes—sidewalls can be sized and printed to match your frame series and campaign. Separate replenishment is common; keep hook placements and zipper lines consistent by referencing the original BOM. Use artwork guidelines for panel seams and safe zones.
