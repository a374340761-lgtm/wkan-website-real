# International GEO Roadmap

This roadmap prepares future Generative Engine Optimization expansion for WaiKwan Tent. Each language version should be manually reviewed by a fluent speaker or regional market specialist before publication. Do not mass machine-translate pages without checking terminology, buyer intent, measurements, product names, legal wording and contact details.

## Recommended next language folders

- Spanish: `/es/`
- French: `/fr/`
- German: `/de/`
- Portuguese: `/pt/`
- Arabic: `/ar/`
- Japanese: `/ja/`
- Korean: `/ko/`

## Recommended process

1. Start with company profile, manufacturing capabilities, FAQ, contact, and the three main product landing pages.
2. Translate product terminology manually, including canopy tents, beach flags, flag poles, portable display systems, light boxes and OEM/ODM manufacturing terms.
3. Keep each page self-referencing canonical and add hreflang only after the matching translated page is published.
4. Localize buyer questions instead of copying English FAQ wording literally.
5. Validate internal links, JSON-LD, sitemap entries and robots access before launch.
