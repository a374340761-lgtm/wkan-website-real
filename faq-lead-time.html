What is your lead time?

Lead time = production time after artwork approval (not including shipping). Below is how to plan your timeline.

Quick Answer

Typical production is 7–15 days after artwork approval. For urgent events, tell us your deadline and destination — we can recommend the fastest production + shipping method.

What affects lead time?

  • Order quantity and number of designs.
  • Printing complexity (full color, double-sided, special finishing).
  • Hardware availability (stock frames/poles can be faster).
  • Artwork readiness (missing bleed/fonts can delay).

How to avoid delays

  • Send AI/PDF vector files; outline fonts; provide pantone references if needed.
  • Confirm sizes and accessories before production starts.
  • Choose shipping method early (express/air/sea).
