What is your MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)?

MOQ depends on product model, printing method, and whether you need custom hardware or only custom graphics.

Quick Answer

We support low MOQ for samples and flexible MOQs for bulk orders. Share your product type, size, quantity target and market — we will propose the most economical MOQ.

Details

  • Printed fabric items (canopies/walls/flag fabrics) may have MOQ based on printing setup and fabric type.
  • Hardware (frames, poles, bases) can sometimes ship from stock — MOQ is usually lower.
  • Mixed orders (same model, multiple designs) are often possible; MOQ is calculated per design or per model.

How to start small (recommended)

  • Order 1 sample set to confirm size, materials, and printing.
  • Approve photos or a physical sample, then start bulk production.
