Do you offer samples?

Samples help confirm size, materials and printing before bulk production.

Quick Answer

Yes. We can provide samples for tents, flags and display systems. Sample charges depend on model/printing; shipping is usually paid by the buyer.

Typical sample flow

  1. Confirm product model, size, and accessories.
  2. Send artwork file (AI/PDF recommended).
  3. We produce the sample and send photos for confirmation.
  4. Ship sample by express, then start bulk after approval.

What to check on a sample

  • Size and fitting (frame/pole compatibility).
  • Printing clarity and color expectations.
  • Stitching/finishing and packing method.
