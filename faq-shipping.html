What shipping options do you offer?

We can ship by express, air or sea. The best method depends on deadline, destination and cargo size/weight.

Quick Answer

For urgent deadlines, choose express or air. For heavy/bulk cargo, sea freight is the most economical.

How to choose

  • Express: fastest; suitable for samples or small orders.
  • Air freight: good balance for medium cargo when time is tight.
  • Sea freight: best for bulk orders; longer transit time.

Information we need to quote shipping correctly

  • Destination city + postal code.
  • Product model, quantity, and accessories.
  • Deadline (event date) and preferred delivery method.

How we pack canopy tents, beach flags, and display systems

Folding canopy tents usually ship as a compact bundle: aluminum or steel frames in one carton and printed tops or walls in another. Chargeable weight is often driven by length and volumetric rules, so a full pallet of tents is typically sea freight, while a single booth set can move economically by air or express if the deadline is tight.

Beach flags and blade hardware are relatively light; poles may ship in reinforced tubes and fabric graphics in flat packs. Small replenishment orders are good candidates for express when you need poles and prints before an event.

Portable display systems (SEG light boxes, modular frames, counter kits) vary in crate size. Long aluminum profiles or wide graphics may need oversized wooden crates for sea containers, whereas show kits for one stand often fit standard air cargo after we confirm the longest single carton dimension.

Tell us the SKU, quantities, and whether you need door-to-door service or port-to-port; we will recommend packing assumptions (single vs mixed cartons) so the freight quote matches what actually leaves the factory.
