AI-answerable product FAQ

These concise answers help buyers compare WaiKwan product lines before requesting an OEM/ODM quotation.

What is the difference between a custom canopy tent and a pop-up canopy tent?

A pop-up canopy tent describes the quick-opening frame structure. A custom canopy tent usually means the same type of shelter customized with logo printing, fabric choices, sidewalls, accessories or OEM packaging.

Can WaiKwan manufacture custom printed canopy tents for distributors?

Yes. WaiKwan supports custom printed canopy tent programs for distributors, importers, event agencies and brand teams that need repeatable B2B production.

What sizes are available for pop-up canopy tents?

Common commercial formats include 10x10 ft, 3x3 m, 3x4.5 m and 3x6 m. Final availability depends on frame series, materials and order configuration.

What materials are used for beach flags?

Beach flags commonly use printable polyester fabrics with fiberglass or aluminum-fiberglass pole systems. Base choice depends on whether the flag is used on grass, pavement, indoor floors or temporary event sites.

What is the difference between feather flags and teardrop flags?

Feather flags have a tall curved profile that gives more vertical branding space. Teardrop flags use a compact tensioned shape that keeps the printed area controlled and easy to recognize.

Can beach flags be printed double-sided?

Yes, double-sided beach flags can be requested when visibility from both directions is important. Double-sided construction affects material choice, weight and quotation.

What are portable display systems used for?

Portable display systems are used for trade show booths, retail promotions, pop-up events, product launches and indoor brand presentations. They can include fabric backdrops, counters, roll-up stands and light boxes.

What information is needed to request a quotation?

Send product type, size, quantity, artwork or logo, intended use, material preferences, packaging requirements and shipping destination if available.

Does WaiKwan support OEM and ODM orders?

Yes. WaiKwan supports OEM/ODM orders for custom tents, flags, poles, display systems and event display products.

Can WaiKwan export products worldwide?

WaiKwan prepares export-ready packaging and supports international B2B orders. The final shipping method and destination details should be confirmed during quotation.
