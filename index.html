What does Wai Kwan Tent manufacture?

Wai Kwan Tent is a China-based OEM/ODM manufacturer of custom canopy tents, pop-up canopy tents, beach flags, flag poles, portable display systems, light boxes and event display products. The company serves global B2B buyers including distributors, event companies, promotional product suppliers, importers and brands that need custom printing, export packing and repeatable production programs.
