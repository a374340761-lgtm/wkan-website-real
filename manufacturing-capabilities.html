Manufacturing Capabilities for OEM/ODM Event Display Products

How WaiKwan supports custom printed canopy tents, beach flags, portable displays and export-ready B2B orders.

OEM/ODM service process

WaiKwan works with buyers that need repeatable manufacturing rather than one-off retail purchases. The process starts with product requirements and artwork, then moves through material confirmation, quotation, proof or sample approval, production, packing and shipment preparation.

  1. Send product type, size, quantity and artwork.
  2. Confirm material, frame or pole system and printing method.
  3. Receive quotation with product configuration and packing assumptions.
  4. Approve design proof or sample when needed.
  5. Start production after order confirmation.
  6. Prepare export packing and shipment documents or labels as agreed.

Canopy Tent Frames

Buyers can discuss aluminum or steel frame options, common commercial sizes, sidewalls, bags and weights based on use frequency and price target.

Fabric & Printing

Canopy, flag and display graphics can be quoted with suitable fabric and print methods. Share artwork and color references early to avoid proofing delays.

Flags, Poles & Bases

Beach flag projects should specify shape, height, single- or double-sided print, pole system, base type, carry bag and outdoor use conditions.

Portable Displays

Display system orders can include fabric backdrops, counters, roll-up stands, light boxes and carrying solutions for trade show kits.

Quote information checklist

  • Product type and target use, such as outdoor canopy, beach flag kit or trade show display.
  • Size, quantity, frame or pole preference and fabric requirements.
  • Artwork files, logo, Pantone references and print coverage.
  • Packaging requirements, carton labels, distributor branding or kit bundling.
  • Destination, preferred shipping method and deadline if available.

Manufacturing capability FAQ

Does WaiKwan support OEM and ODM orders?

Yes. WaiKwan supports OEM/ODM programs where buyers define product type, size, materials, artwork, packaging and recurring order requirements.

What artwork files are preferred?

Vector AI or PDF files are preferred. Fonts should be outlined, images embedded or linked at print quality, and Pantone or color references should be supplied when color control matters.

Can WaiKwan prepare products for export shipment?

Yes. The website describes export-ready packing, carton labels, product bundling and shipment preparation for bulk orders.
