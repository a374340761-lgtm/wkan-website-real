伟群帐篷亮相上海 APPPEXPO 2026 展会 Wai Kwan Tent Showcased at APPPEXPO 2026 in Shanghai

展示最新展示器材产品，与客户面对面交流，传递我们的制造实力。 Presenting our latest display solutions, meeting customers face-to-face, and sharing our manufacturing strength on site.

March 2026 Shanghai, China

展会概况

2026年3月，伟群帐篷参加了在上海举办的 APPPEXPO 2026 展会，并在现场展示了公司一系列最新的展示与品牌宣传解决方案。这次展会为我们提供了一个与客户面对面交流、更加直观展示产品、并进一步加强与不同市场客户及合作伙伴联系的宝贵机会。

Exhibition Overview

In March 2026, Wai Kwan Tent exhibited at APPPEXPO 2026 in Shanghai, presenting our latest display and branding solutions. The show gave us the opportunity to meet visitors in person, walk through product details on the stand, and strengthen relationships with customers and partners across regions.

现场展示产品

在本次展会中，我们重点展示了沙滩旗、旗杆、底座、活动展示器材及相关定制品牌宣传产品。这些产品广泛应用于户外广告、活动推广、零售陈列、展览展示以及商业品牌宣传等场景。

Products on Display

On display were beach flags, flag poles, bases, event display hardware, and custom branding solutions. Typical applications include outdoor advertising, promotions, retail, exhibitions, and commercial branding.

现场客户交流

展会期间，许多到访客户对我们的产品质量、结构细节、便携性以及工厂制造能力表现出浓厚兴趣。通过面对面的交流，我们也进一步了解了不同地区客户对产品与服务的实际需求。

Meeting Customers On Site

Visitors asked about product quality, structural details, portability, and our manufacturing capabilities. Conversations on the stand helped us clarify market needs and regional expectations.

现场沟通与接待 — 感谢每一位到访的朋友。 On-site communication — thank you to everyone who visited our booth.

未来展望

对我们而言，展会不仅仅是展示产品的平台，更是建立信任、交流想法、持续改进的重要机会。作为一家专注于帐篷、旗帜、展示系统以及 OEM / ODM 生产的制造商，伟群帐篷将继续为全球客户提供更可靠的产品与更高效的服务。

感谢每一位在展会期间到访并与我们交流的朋友。期待未来与更多客户建立联系并展开合作。

Looking Ahead

Trade shows are not only a venue for product display—they support trust, feedback, and continuous improvement. As an OEM/ODM manufacturer of tents, beach flags, display hardware, and related solutions, Wai Kwan Tent remains focused on reliable products and responsive service for customers worldwide.

Thank you to everyone who visited our booth. We look forward to further collaboration.
