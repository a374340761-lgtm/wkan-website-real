Beach Flag Base Selection Guide

The right base depends on where the flag will stand. Grass, pavement, indoor floors and windy outdoor spaces need different hardware.

Common base choices

  • Ground spike: grass, soil and temporary outdoor events.
  • Cross base: indoor floors and smooth hard ground.
  • Water base: extra stability for pavement and outdoor promotions.
  • Heavy metal base: premium indoor or storefront applications.

Quote checklist

Send flag shape, flag size, pole material, base type, surface condition, quantity and packing requirement.
