WaiKwan at the 139th Canton Fair Phase 3: Custom Tents, Flags & Display Solutions in Guangzhou

Booth photos, buyer conversations and product highlights from WaiKwan Tent at Canton Fair Phase 3.

May 2026 Guangzhou, China

Meeting Global Buyers at the 139th Canton Fair Phase 3

WaiKwan Tent joined the 139th Canton Fair Phase 3 in Guangzhou to meet international buyers looking for practical event display, outdoor advertising and brand promotion solutions. The exhibition gave our team a direct way to introduce our factory capabilities, explain product options and understand what buyers need for local markets.

At the booth, visitors discussed event setup speed, printed brand visibility, packaging, wholesale programs and how different display products can work together at trade shows, retail promotions, sports events and outdoor campaigns. Many conversations focused on matching product structure, printing methods and accessories to real project requirements.

For buyers comparing suppliers after the Canton Fair, this page provides a reference for WaiKwan booth photos, displayed product categories and common questions about custom manufacturing.

What We Showcased at Our Booth

Our Canton Fair booth highlighted connected event display solutions, including custom canopy tents for trade shows, pop up canopy tents, advertising tents, beach flags and flag poles, inflatable arches, race gates, fabric pop-up backdrops, tension fabric counters, portable display systems for exhibitions, light boxes, event furniture and folding tables.

Buyers could compare tent frames, printed graphics, flag hardware, display counters and lighting products in one setting, making it easier to plan complete promotional kits. For product selection, buyers can browse all WaiKwan products, view custom tent products, compare flag and pole systems, or explore display systems.

Client Meetings and Buyer Conversations

Many buyers visited the booth to discuss logo printing, product combinations, bulk order planning and export packaging. These conversations help our team recommend the right custom event display solution instead of only offering a single product.

Common Questions Buyers Asked Us

Can WaiKwan customize tents with our logo?

Yes. We can print logos, brand colors, roof graphics, valances and sidewalls for custom canopy tents and related event kits.

What products are suitable for events and exhibitions?

Custom canopy tents, beach flags, inflatable arches, race gates, pop-up backdrops, counters, light boxes, folding tables and portable display systems are commonly used together.

Do you support wholesale and bulk orders?

Yes. WaiKwan supports wholesale orders, distributor programs and OEM/ODM production for international buyers.

Can buyers request a catalogue or quotation after the Canton Fair?

Yes. Share your product type, size, quantity, artwork and target delivery timing so our team can prepare catalogue details and a quotation.

What makes WaiKwan suitable for international buyers?

Our team combines factory-direct product knowledge, custom printing support, export packaging and responsive communication for global event and advertising markets.
