WaiKwan at the 140th Canton Fair Phase 3: Inflatable Arches, Sofas & Event Displays

Exhibition completed · October 31–November 4, 2026 · Booth 12.1F29–30.

October 31–November 4, 2026 Guangzhou, China

A complete event display in one booth

WaiKwan completed its participation in the 140th Canton Fair Phase 3, held October 31–November 4, 2026 in Guangzhou, presenting our newly added inflatable arches and inflatable sofas alongside canopy frames, beach flags, light boxes and portable display equipment. The booth brought these products together to show how matching graphics can connect an entrance, product display and seating area.

Visitors can use the photographs below to identify the products they saw and send our team their preferred size, quantity, printing requirements and delivery destination.

New inflatable arches and sofas

Our inflatable arches create a branded focal point for event entrances, promotional spaces and exhibition layouts. Share the intended opening size, installation location and artwork so we can recommend a suitable configuration and confirm the anchoring and inflation accessories required.

Inflatable sofas provide a coordinated seating area for exhibitions, brand activations and event lounges. The show display pairs matching printed seating with inflatable structures. Tell us the number of seats, preferred arrangement and branding needs; dimensions, materials, accessories and packing are confirmed with the quotation.

Explore inflatable productsRequest arch and sofa details

Flags, frames and illuminated displays

Beach flags add height and visibility around the booth, while displayed frame sections let buyers compare construction details. Light boxes and literature stands complete the presentation. These products can be discussed individually or as a coordinated event kit with consistent artwork.

From preparation to the exhibition floor

Ordering and customization · MOQ usually 1 piece

Most products are available from 1 piece, making it possible to start with a single display or a small event kit. Final minimum quantities depend on the selected model and customization requirements and are confirmed in the quotation. Distributor and larger project enquiries are welcome.

For a detailed proposal, send the product name or a booth photo, required dimensions, order quantity, logo or artwork, delivery destination and target arrival date. Our team will confirm the specification, printing layout, included accessories, packing and production timing before the order proceeds.
