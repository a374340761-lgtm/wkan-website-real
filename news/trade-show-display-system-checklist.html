Trade Show Display System Procurement Checklist

A complete booth kit may include a fabric backwall, counter, roll-up banner, SEG light box, printed graphics, lights, bags and export cartons.

Build the kit by booth size

Start with booth width and wall height. Then select backwall type, counter size, lighting and transport method.

Artwork and packing

Confirm graphic dimensions, bleed, color references and whether cartons need private labels. For repeat programs, keep spare graphics and hardware parts available.
