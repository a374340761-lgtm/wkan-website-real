Pop Up Canopy Tent Manufacturer

Factory-direct canopy pop up tent systems for distributors, event agencies, brand owners and OEM/ODM export programs.

WaiKwan manufactures pop up canopy tents for B2B buyers who need durable frames, vivid custom graphics and reliable export packing. Our canopy pop up tent programs cover 10x10 pop up canopy tents, metric 3x3 m event tents, larger 3x4.5 m and 3x6 m sizes, sidewalls, bags, weights and spare parts. As a factory-direct OEM/ODM supplier, we help distributors and agencies choose frame thickness, roof fabric, printing method, carton labels and accessory bundles for repeat orders. The result is a complete outdoor promotion product system that can be sold as a branded kit, event rental package or wholesale catalog line.
