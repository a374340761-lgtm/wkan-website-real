Frame Selection

Standard Black is a separate flat-tube frame model. Do not apply the weight, wall thickness or performance specifications of the 30 square-tube or 40/50 hexagonal frames. Compare brace profile, joints and canopy compatibility.

Before Requesting a Quote

Specify Standard Black, required size, quantity and destination when requesting a quote. Weight varies with size and configuration. Confirm frame material, tube dimensions, wall thickness, packing, canopy compatibility and anchoring requirements in writing.
