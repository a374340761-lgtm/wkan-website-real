<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>SIGN CHINA</strong> was founded in 2003. After more than 20 years of development, the brand has become famous all over the world and is recognized by the industry as the &#8220;Oscar&#8221; event of the global advertising industry. For 15 years, it has welcomed overseas professional buyers from more than 100 countries and regions around the world. Every year, thousands of high-quality Chinese manufacturers are selected to participate in the exhibition services, which are highly recognized and praised by international buyers (especially European and American buyers).</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>SIGN CHINA</strong> focuses on advertising and is based on the East China Advertising Industry Base. It aims to create a global one-stop procurement event for advertising and digital products such as printing/laser/engraving, advertising materials, sign light boxes, retail display, exhibition equipment, LED advertising light sources and lighting, LED display screens and digital signage!</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Together with the official SIGN CHINA mall, the offline and online integration provides the best trading platform for the industry to expand global business 365 days a year.<br>Venue: Shanghai New International Expo Centre</p>
