帐篷类型与规格指南 Tent Types & Specifications

This hub explains WaiKwan folding canopy tent series: 30 (square-tube) and 40/50 (hexagon) frames, standard metric sizes, heat-transfer printing on tops and walls, and how to request factory-direct pricing. It is built for importers, distributors, event companies, and display buyers comparing OEM or wholesale tent programs. Folding Canopy Tent Types

30 Series Pop Up Canopy Tent

30 mm square-tube frame (iron or aluminum). Standard promotional sizes from 1.5×1.5 m to 3×6 m. Economical choice for high-volume programs.

Compare Tent Series

30 series targets cost-sensitive, high-rotation use with smaller tube diameter. 40 series balances weight and rigidity for typical trade show and roadshow use. 50 series is positioned for the largest available folding footprints and the stiffest frame in this group. All three support custom graphics; MOQ from 1 unit and typical production 7–15 days (carton export packing). Custom Canopy Tent Manufacturer explains full OEM workflow.

10x10 Canopy Tent Options

A 3×3 m canopy is the metric equivalent of a 10×10 ft market reference. 10×10 / 3×3 options exist across 30, 40, and 50 series with different frame grades—compare each series page before locking specifications. For US-market wording, also see 10x10 pop up canopy tent and Browse All Tents in the live catalog.

Common Tent Sizes

Standard metric footprints include 1.5×1.5 m through 4×8 m depending on series. Each series page lists models, weights, and can pair with 30 Series, 40 Series, or 50 Series detail tables.

Printing & Customization

Heat transfer printing is available for canopy tops, half walls, and full walls. WaiKwan coordinates frame production and fabric finishing in one workflow for repeat B2B orders. Canopy tent printing options (topic) complement this technical hub.
