Custom Canopy Tents — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for buyers; full detail in our FAQ library.

What sizes are available for custom canopy tents?

We supply common commercial footprints including 3×3 m, 3×4.5 m, and 3×6 m styles among others. Exact availability depends on frame series—use tent category listings or ask for a matrix during quoting.

Can you print logos on all canopy panels?

Roof and valance branding is standard for many custom printed canopy tents; sidewall printing may vary by fabric and seam layout. Share your layout early—we outline bleed and safe zones in our artwork FAQ.

Do you offer aluminum and steel frames?

Yes. Aluminum suits frequent transport; steel can reduce unit cost for budget-led programs. Specify intended use when contacting us so we recommend the right pop up canopy tents frame.

What is the MOQ for custom canopy tents?

MOQ depends on print scope, fabric, and accessories. See MOQ guidance and include quantity targets in your inquiry for an accurate line item.

Do you support OEM orders for distributors?

We operate as an OEM canopy tent manufacturer for B2B partners—private-label packaging and repeat-order consistency can be discussed with our team via contact.

Can sidewalls and accessories be included?

Yes—sidewalls, weights, and related hardware can be quoted with your frame. Mention venue requirements (e.g., fire retardancy) when requesting printed event tents packages.
