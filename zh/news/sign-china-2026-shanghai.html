SIGN CHINA 2026 · SHANGHAI

上海国际广告展
充气新品，现场呈现New inflatables.
On show in Shanghai.

伟群帐篷携三角充气帐篷、充气桌子及充气椅亮相上海国际广告展。WaiKwan presents inflatable V-shaped tents, tables and chairs at SIGN CHINA 2026.

2026年9月15–17日 · 上海新国际博览中心15–17 September 2026 · Shanghai New International Expo Centre

从展位搭建到产品展示From booth setup to product showcase

本次上海国际广告展，伟群帐篷将帐篷、旗帜、充气拱门及展示柱组合成完整的品牌展示空间。现场重点带来三角 / V 型充气帐篷、充气桌子和充气椅，为展位接待、品牌活动与休息洽谈区提供更多搭配选择。At SIGN CHINA 2026, WaiKwan brought canopy tents, flags, inflatable arches and display columns together in a branded exhibition space. Our new inflatable V-shaped tent, table and seating range adds more possibilities for reception areas, brand events and visitor lounges.

本次新品New products on display

01 / 三角 / V 型充气帐篷01 / Inflatable triangular / V-shaped tent

闭气式充气结构，充气完成后无需持续连接气泵。采用 400D（220g）PU 涂层涤纶布、0.3mm TPU 内胆，支持热升华全彩印刷，可选配电泵。A sealed-air structure that does not require a continuously running blower after inflation. Made with 400D (220g) PU-coated polyester and a 0.3mm TPU bladder, with full-colour dye-sublimation printing and an optional electric pump.

02 / 充气桌子02 / Inflatable table

尺寸为 60 × 42 cm，可与充气椅组合布置洽谈及休息区。现场桌椅采用统一的品牌画面，与充气帐篷搭配，形成完整的接待空间。Available in a 60 × 42 cm size, the inflatable table pairs with our seating to create a meeting or lounge area. The coordinated graphics shown on the stand bring the table, chairs and inflatable tent together as a complete reception space.

03 / 单人及双人充气椅03 / Single-seat and two-seat inflatable chairs

采用 0.5mm PVC 内胆与 220g 数码印花布料，提供单人和双人两种款式，适合搭配充气桌子用于展会接待及活动休息区。Built with a 0.5mm PVC bladder and 220g digitally printed fabric, with single-seat and two-seat options for exhibition reception and event lounge areas.
