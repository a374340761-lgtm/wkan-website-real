Tension Fabric Backwall Manufacturer

A tension fabric backwall pairs a lightweight aluminum frame with a zippered or SEG-style printed skin, producing a large fabric backdrop display with minimal seams. For exhibitors, the same system becomes a repeatable trade show backdrop that ships in compact cartons and assembles with basic tooling.

WaiKwan manufactures tension fabric systems for straight runs, curved profiles and modular layouts—aligned to booth sizes, brand graphics and freight constraints.
